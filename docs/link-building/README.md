# Link Building Kit — 2026 Strategy for Naturally Restful

This is the off-site half of the 2026 link building guide, applied to this site.
The on-site half is already built: the Reality Index (`/data`), sleep statistics
hub (`/sleep-statistics`), press & citing page (`/press`), llms.txt, AI-crawler
robots policy, and entity JSON-LD schema.

## Positioning (read first)

Our unfair advantage is **original data**. Almost nobody in the sleep-supplement
niche has a citable dataset. Every tactic below exists to put the Reality Index
and the statistics hub in front of people who *need a source to cite*.

Golden rule for 2026: every outreach email must offer something the recipient's
article gains by using — a number, a table, a methodology. Never "would you
link to my site."

## What NOT to do (2026 rules)

- No bought links, PBNs, or link exchanges at scale — actively devalued.
- No mass AI-generated outreach — one personalized email beats fifty blasted.
- No exact-match anchor requests ("best sleep supplement") — natural anchors
  only (site name, URL, or descriptive phrase).
- Don't try to manipulate AI Overviews/AI Mode — classified as spam since
  May 2026. Just make content citable and let it happen.

## Prospecting filters

A prospect qualifies when ALL are true:
1. Topically about sleep, stress, supplements, health, or productivity.
2. Real traffic (check: pages on the site rank for something).
3. Editorial links exist (their articles link out to sources).
4. Not a pure affiliate landing page or content farm.

Log every prospect in `prospect-tracker.csv`. Follow the cadence in
`90-day-calendar.md`.

## KPIs to review monthly

- New referring domains (GSC + Ahrefs free tools).
- Referral traffic from citations.
- AI answer citations: search ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews
  monthly for "best sleep supplement," "glycine for sleep," "sleep supplement
  statistics," and log whether naturallyrestful.xyz appears.
- Rankings for the statistics hub (target: writers finding us for
  "sleep statistics" + source).
