# MaxBounty Phone Interview — Complete Preparation Kit

## Your 20-second opening (memorize this):

> "I run an organic SEO content site, naturallyrestful.xyz, in the sleep-supplement niche targeting US and UK readers. I publish research-cited review and comparison articles and promote offers contextually inside the content. I also have hands-on Pinterest and Facebook marketing experience."

## What they'll ask (and your answers):

### "Tell me about your traffic sources"
> "100% organic search — SEO content marketing. No paid traffic, no incentivized traffic, no email spam. The site is verified in Google Search Console with a submitted sitemap, and all URLs are indexed through Bing's IndexNow API."

### "How do you plan to promote our offers?"
> "Contextually within my content. For example, if I have an article about ashwagandha for stress, I'd place a relevant health offer inline — not as a banner, but as a genuine recommendation within the article's recommendations section."

### "What's your experience level?"
> "I've been building this site for the past two weeks with consistent content output — 41 pages of original, research-cited guides. I was recently accepted into the SellHealth affiliate network, which validated my content quality and approach."

### "What verticals are you interested in?"
> "Health and beauty — specifically nutra lead-gen and CPL offers. My audience is actively researching sleep and stress supplements, so health-focused offers are the natural fit."

### "Are you working with other networks?"
> "Yes, SellHealth recently approved my application. I'm also in the review process with iHerb. I'm looking for 2-3 quality networks, not spreading thin across dozens."

### "How much traffic do you get?"
> "The site is new — about two weeks old — so I'm in the Google sandbox period. Current traffic is primarily crawlers and early discovery. I'm building content volume now because that's what drives organic search rankings once the sandbox lifts."

## Rules for the call:

1. **Be honest about being new** — they have beginner-friendly offers
2. **Emphasize content quality** — "research-cited" is your differentiator
3. **Never say "I'll figure it out"** — always have a specific answer
4. **Mention SellHealth approval** — it's social proof that another network vetted you
5. **Sound confident, not desperate** — you have a live revenue stream already
6. **Ask questions back** — "What health CPL offers would you recommend for a content site like mine?"
