Ranked for one job — **sleep** — not marketing. The scoring: sleep-specific human evidence, tolerability at bedtime, and price per effective dose.

## 1. Magnesium Glycinate (bisglycinate) — the clear pick

- **Sleep evidence:** the best of any form (modest, consistent trial effects on sleep quality — [full analysis](/articles/magnesium-glycinate-vs-citrate-for-sleep))
- **Tolerance:** excellent — the gentlest commonly available form
- **Price:** ★★★★☆ — reasonable; [how to pick an honest brand](/articles/best-magnesium-glycinate-brands-coa)
- **Dose:** 200–400 mg elemental, nightly, [timed like this](/articles/magnesium-before-bed-timing-and-dosage)

## 2. Magnesium Taurate — the quiet runner-up

- **Sleep evidence:** thin directly, but taurate has plausible calming-adjacent signals and good tolerability
- **Tolerance:** very good
- **Price:** ★★★☆☆
- Honest verdict: a fine Plan B if glycinate disagrees with you, though it lacks glycinate's trial base.

## 3. Magnesium Threonate — premium price, sleep-neutral

- **Sleep evidence:** essentially none specific to sleep; its story is cognition (mostly animal data — [our honest takedown](/articles/magnesium-threonate-worth-the-premium))
- **Price per mg elemental:** the worst on the shelf
- Verdict: don't buy it *for sleep*.

## Skip entirely

- **Magnesium oxide** — cheap, ~4% absorption; it's a laxative that occasionally masquerades as a supplement
- **"Blended" formulas** — oxide hidden behind a glycinate label ("glycinate buffered with oxide") — read the back panel
- **Citrate as a bedtime choice** — well-absorbed but the laxative edge makes it a daytime form at best

## Bottom line

For sleep: **glycinate, 200–400 mg, tested brand, consistent nights.** Everything else is either a specialist tool or a marketing story. Full context in the [master comparison](/best-sleep-supplements).
