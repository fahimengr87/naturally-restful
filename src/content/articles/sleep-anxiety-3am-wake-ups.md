The most common sleep complaint we hear isn't "I can't fall asleep" — it's "**I wake at 3 a.m. with my mind on fire.**" Sleep-maintenance trouble has different causes than sleep-onset trouble, and the supplement hierarchy differs too.

## First: what 3 a.m. wake-ups usually mean

- **Alcohol** — the classic: sedates early, rebounds as it metabolizes. The single most common cause. ([The non-supplement fixes](/articles/natural-sleep-aids-not-supplements) cover this honestly.)
- **Stress-cortisol timing** — a stressed system can spike arousal mid-night
- **Blood sugar dips** — especially late-evening sugar or skipped dinner
- **Sleep apnea** — if you wake gasping or with morning headaches, that's a doctor, not a supplement
- **Age** — sleep architecture lightens; fewer deep cycles means easier awakenings

Rule out the first and fourth before buying anything.

## The supplement hierarchy for sleep *maintenance*

1. **Glycine, 3 g before bed** — trials showed better *subjective sleep quality* including fewer groggy wake-ups — [the full dosing piece](/articles/glycine-for-sleep-the-3-gram-dose)
2. **Magnesium glycinate nightly** — the slow regulatory option; [protocol here](/articles/magnesium-before-bed-timing-and-dosage)
3. **Ashwagandha (if stress-driven), 6–8 weeks** — works upstream on the stress system — [evidence and exclusions](/articles/ashwagandha-for-stress-what-the-research-says)
4. **L-theanine at wake-up** — 100–200 mg *when you wake at 3 a.m.* is a reasonable, non-sedating strategy many report helps quiet the spiral back to sleep

## What NOT to do

Don't take melatonin for 3 a.m. wake-ups — it's a clock-shifter, not a maintenance aid ([why](/articles/melatonin-most-people-take-too-much)). And don't chase "PM" blends stacking five sedatives — morning grogginess trades places with the wake-up.

## Bottom line

Fix alcohol, snoring, and late sugar first. Then glycine + magnesium is the evidence-friendly pair for this exact pattern — and if the 3 a.m. mind-racing is really daytime anxiety uninvited into the night, [that comparison](/articles/ashwagandha-vs-l-theanine) is your next stop.
