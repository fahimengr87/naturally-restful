Every few months 5-HTP trends as a mood-and-sleep supplement, usually with a diagram showing the elegant pathway: 5-HTP → serotonin → melatonin. The diagram is real. The evidence and safety story are where honesty is required.

## The mechanism (the selling point)

5-HTP is the direct precursor to serotonin, which converts to melatonin. Take the precursor, theoretically raise the outputs. It's the same logic that made tryptophan famous — 5-HTP is just one step further down the chain.

## The evidence (the weak point)

- Human sleep trials for 5-HTP are **old, small, and inconsistent** — mostly from the 1980s–90s, with mixed results on sleep quality and no modern high-quality replication.
- Unlike [magnesium](/articles/magnesium-before-bed-timing-and-dosage), [glycine](/articles/glycine-for-sleep-the-3-gram-dose), or even [tart cherry](/articles/tart-cherry-juice-for-sleep), nothing in the modern literature anchors 5-HTP as a sleep aid.
- A related, documented effect: 5-HTP can **suppress REM sleep** somewhat (increasing deep-sleep share in some studies) — sometimes marketed as a benefit, but chronically blunted REM is not obviously a good trade.

## The risk (the deal-breaker for some)

**5-HTP plus SSRIs/SNRIs/MAOIs or tramadol stacks serotonin — serotonin syndrome risk is real and documented.** This is not a theoretical interaction; it's the same logic as our [antidepressants checklist](/articles/supplements-and-antidepressants-checklist): anyone on psychiatric medication does not take 5-HTP without their prescriber's explicit sign-off. Additional footnotes: it can also interact with carb levodopa therapy (Parkinson's) and cause GI upset at higher doses.

## Bottom line

Mechanistically pretty, clinically thin, interactively serious. If the pathway diagram is what attracts you, [glycine](/articles/glycine-for-sleep-the-3-gram-dose) offers a better-studied amino-acid story without the serotonin stacking, and [melatonin itself](/articles/melatonin-most-people-take-too-much) — taken correctly, at 0.5–1 mg — is the endpoint 5-HTP promises to reach by detour. Our [comparison](/best-sleep-supplements) leaves 5-HTP off the ranked list deliberately; this page is the receipt.

**Key studies:** earliest positive sleep findings date to Birdsall 1998 (review, low rigor by modern standards) · modern literature: no high-quality RCT support for sleep · FDA/EMA interaction warnings regarding serotonergic co-administration.
