Here's the page most supplement sites hope you skip: the sleep interventions with *stronger* evidence than anything in a bottle — all free or nearly so.

## 1. CBT-I: the gold standard nobody mentions first

**Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia** outperforms sleeping pills in head-to-head trials — durably, without dependency. It's the first-line recommendation in every major clinical guideline. Digital programs and apps (several well-studied) deliver much of the benefit. If insomnia is chronic, start here, not at the supplement aisle.

## 2. Light: the master switch

- **Morning sunlight within an hour of waking, 10–30 minutes** — the single most effective circadian anchor. Free, immediate, underrated.
- **Dim the house 2 hours before bed**; screens on night mode at minimum. Bright evening light delays your clock exactly when you don't want it delayed.

## 3. Consistent schedule — including weekends

Your circadian system reads *consistency* as its primary instruction. A stable wake time does more for sleep quality than most chemistry. Sleep-late weekends create "social jetlag" — Monday 3 a.m. is born Saturday morning.

## 4. Temperature

Sleep onset rides a **drop in core body temperature** (the same mechanism behind [glycine's effect](/articles/glycine-for-sleep-the-3-gram-dose)). Cool room (18–19°C), warm shower an hour before bed — the post-shower cooldown is a natural sleep trigger.

## 5. Caffeine curfew

Caffeine's half-life is ~5–6 hours; a 4 p.m. coffee is still one-quarter active at midnight. Cutting caffeine after noon fixes more "insomnia" than any supplement. ([L-theanine](/articles/l-theanine-for-a-racing-mind) can smooth what remains.)

## 6. The bed = sleep rule

If awake beyond ~20 minutes, leave the bed, do something calm in dim light, return sleepy. This retrains the brain's bed-sleep association — a core CBT-I move.

## 7. Alcohol honesty

Alcohol sedates then fragments — the 3 a.m. rebound wake-up many readers seek supplements for is often just metabolized wine.

## Bottom line

Every supplement on this site sits *on top of* these fundamentals — that's how we frame them. Fix the free stuff first, and when something like [magnesium](/articles/magnesium-before-bed-timing-and-dosage) is added, it has an actual foundation to work on.
